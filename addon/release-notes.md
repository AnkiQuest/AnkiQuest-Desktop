The AnkiQuest add-on for Anki: streaks, friends, leaderboards and moments, on the same account as [AnkiQuest for Android](https://github.com/AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Android).

**Study companion.** Choose your study companion under Settings → Account. Ankilope joins you when you reach a level, with a short reveal; "Reduce motion" under App and privacy shows a fade instead. Aki greets you once after you install or update the add-on. Your companion's pose follows your streak: asleep, happy, on fire, sad, hopeful, frozen or neglected.

**Today.** The streak flame stays unlit until today counts. Friends' study stays hidden until you upload your first reviews.

**Sharing.** Choose who gets each moment you share, including older moments, completed decks and your weekly place. Share pictures are drawn on your computer, so they work offline, and streak pictures change with the length of the streak. When Undo of a share fails, the add-on says so.

**Deck alerts.** The inbox names the sender of each deck alert and has "Stop deck alerts from" for each sender. The daily deck-sharing prompt is separate from the other notifications.

**Sync settings.** Rank and streak notifications, deck markers, session recap, goal celebrations and the review heatmap stay the same on all your devices, Android included. Turn it off under Settings → App and privacy → Sync settings.

**Conquered cards.** Conquest details count learning days, as on Android.

**Rate and report.** Tools → AnkiQuest ends with Rate AnkiQuest (on AnkiWeb or GitHub), AnkiQuest changelog, which shows these notes, and Report a bug. Settings credits Shigeyuki for Anki-tan.

Study uploads contain review history (card id, timestamp, previous and new interval, time taken and review type) and deck progress, never card content.
